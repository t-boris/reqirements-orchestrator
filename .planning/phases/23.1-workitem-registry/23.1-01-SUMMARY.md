## Decisions Made

1. **Enum inheritance from str** - WorkItemType and WorkItemStatus inherit from (str, Enum) for JSON serialization compatibility, following existing IntentType/PendingAction patterns
2. **Nullable Jira fields** - jira_key, jira_sync_at, jira_fingerprint all nullable to support drafts that don't yet have Jira backing
3. **JSONB for complex fields** - facts and jira_fingerprint stored as JSONB for flexibility, parsed in _row_to_workitem()
4. **Three indexes** - channel_id (list queries), jira_key (sync lookups), parent_id (hierarchy queries) for performance
