## Decisions Made

1. **Queue only for blocking actions:** Only queue requests when pending_action is one of:
   - WAITING_APPROVAL
   - WAITING_SCOPE_CHOICE
   - WAITING_UPDATE_CONFIRM
   - WAITING_STORY_EDIT
   - WAITING_DECISION_EDIT

   This prevents interleaving during multi-step flows while allowing normal concurrent messages.

2. **Non-blocking participation tracking:** Errors in _record_participation() are logged but don't fail the request. This ensures participation tracking doesn't break the user experience.

3. **Max 2 mentions per message:** The limit_mentions() function caps @mentions at 2 to prevent notification spam.
