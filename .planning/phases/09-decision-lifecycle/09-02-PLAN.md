<objective>
Add Jira notification when a decision is deprecated or superseded.

Purpose: Currently, deprecating a decision is a local-only operation — Jira has no idea the decision was reversed. If a decision was projected as a Jira comment, the team using Jira sees stale information. This plan adds a follow-up Jira comment when a decision is deprecated, noting the supersession and linking to the replacement.

Output: Jira sync service posts "Decision Superseded" comment when a decision is deprecated.
</objective>
